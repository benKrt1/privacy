Privacy Policy

Swedish Stories Postiz · Last updated: 5 November 2026

Swedish Stories Postiz is a private, self-hosted publishing tool. It is used only by the owner of the "Swedish Stories" channels to schedule and publish their own videos and posts to their own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account. It is not offered to the public and it does not have other users.

What data the app accesses

When the owner connects an account, the app receives access tokens from Google (YouTube) and Meta (Facebook, Instagram), plus basic account information such as the channel, Page or account name, ID and profile picture. The app uses this only to publish content the owner has created and to show the status of those posts.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process personal data of viewers, followers or any other third party.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.
  • It does not use data for advertising or profiling.

Where data is stored

Access tokens and the owner's posts are stored on the owner's own server and are never shared. Use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Data deletion

To delete all data held by this app, disconnect the account in the app or remove the app's access:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Facebook / Instagram: Facebook Settings → Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites) → remove the app.

Once the account is disconnected, the stored tokens and account information are deleted from the server. You can also ask for deletion by email.
